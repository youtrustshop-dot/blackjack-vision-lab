separately, prospectively. R1 still requires legible current player/upcard,
visible phase/enabled controls, relevant numeric provenance, arithmetic and
current-pixel revalidation. It does not certify shoe history (**R2**).

and closed conditional fourth slot. No historical score was revised.

maximum continuity gap **250 ms**. Redrawing, polling or receiving an identical

must never reach current advice. No candidate is promoted by this increment.

at native **1024×768** resolution. Only pixels, capture time and declared geometry
reach recognition; renderer phase, turn, card identity and references do not.
Separate capture/analysis requests let the producer continue during inference.
Default research endpoints are disabled until explicitly configured locally.

session. Opening/closing/reopening a view cannot start another reader or solver.
The popup declares that browser always-on-top is not guaranteed.

is preserved; a usable local observation spends no provider request. References

consumed failure.
